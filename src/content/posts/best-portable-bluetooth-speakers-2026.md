Battery life and water resistance have become table stakes for portable Bluetooth speakers — nearly everything at this point claims both. The real differences between models now come down to audio quality under real conditions, and whether you actually need the extra features (apps, stereo pairing, lossless audio) that come with a higher price tag.

## The budget pick covers the basics well

The Soundcore Select 4 Go doesn't have an app or fine-tuned EQ controls, but the fundamentals — 20 hours of battery, real IP67 waterproofing, and the ability to pair two units for bigger sound — cover what most people actually want from a portable speaker. If you're buying your first Bluetooth speaker or want a beater for the pool or beach, this is a sensible starting point.

## Where the JBL Flip 7 earns its price

The jump to the JBL Flip 7 is mostly about audio quality — it holds up better than the budget pick across bass, mids, and highs, especially at higher volumes where cheaper speakers start to distort. The companion app's EQ customization is also a genuine feature, not just a marketing checkbox, if you like tuning sound to your preference.

## Is the Beats Pill worth the premium over the Flip 7?

Mostly for bass-heavy listening and battery life — 24 hours beats the Flip 7's 16, and the bass response is stronger. The tradeoff is that the Beats Pill struggles more at maximum volume than the JBL does, so if you regularly listen at high volumes outdoors, the Flip 7 may actually hold up better despite costing less.

## Bottom line

Start with the Soundcore if budget matters most and you don't need app customization. Move to the JBL Flip 7 if you want noticeably better sound quality and don't mind a bit more weight. Choose the Beats Pill specifically if bass response and battery life matter more to you than how it performs at max volume.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I connect two of the same speaker for stereo sound?**

Yes, all three of these support pairing two units of the same model for a wider stereo image, though the Soundcore Select 4 Go is specifically marketed around this feature at its price point. Mixing different models together for stereo pairing generally isn't supported.

**How waterproof is "waterproof" for these speakers — can they be submerged?**

An IP67 or IP68 rating means these can handle submersion in shallow water for a limited time (typically up to 30 minutes at around 1 meter depth), not sustained underwater use. They're built for splashes, pool play, and rain, not swimming laps with the speaker attached.

**Do these speakers work as a hands-free speakerphone for calls?**

All three include a microphone for calls, but call quality on portable speakers is generally a secondary feature compared to dedicated speakerphones — expect it to work fine for quick calls in quiet settings, less reliably in windy or noisy outdoor environments.
